## Full description (max 4,000)

Arth turns the bank and UPI messages already on your phone into a complete, private picture of your money. No account to create, no cloud, no ads. Everything stays on your device.

AUTOMATIC TRACKING FROM BANK SMS
Your bank already texts you every time money moves. With your permission, Arth reads those transaction alerts on your phone, works out the amount, merchant, account and balance, and puts each one in a review queue. You approve it, and it lands in your accounts, budgets and reports. Personal messages are ignored, and no SMS ever leaves your phone.
• Works with SMS from major Indian banks, credit cards and UPI apps
• Teach Arth a new message format once with Smart SMS Templates
• Smart Rules categorise repeat merchants for you
• Add cash spends and anything SMS can't catch by hand or by voice

ACCOUNTS AND BALANCES
• Savings accounts, credit cards, wallets, deposits, loans and pension (EPF) in one place
• Month-by-month ledgers with opening and closing balances
• Transfers between your own accounts kept separate from spending
• Reconcile an account against your statement

BUDGETS AND INSIGHTS
• Monthly budgets by category, with spending splits and a monthly summary
• Upcoming dues and reminders for bills, EMIs and card payments
• Spending patterns, month and year comparisons, and a savings rate for your financial year (April to March)
• A cash-flow simulator to test a decision before you make it

LOANS, GOALS AND NET WORTH
• Loan schedules with prepayment planning
• Investment buckets, life milestones and a balance sheet of your net worth
• Hisaab: a family ledger for money lent and borrowed, with split expenses

INVESTMENTS (OPTIONAL)
Connect your own Zerodha Kite, Angel One or Zebpay API key to see holdings alongside everything else. Connections are read-only: Arth never places orders or moves money. Arth is not affiliated with any broker.

ARTH AI (OPTIONAL)
Ask questions about your money in plain English. The AI model is downloaded once and runs entirely on your phone. Built with Llama.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No sign-up and no Arth servers: your data lives in a database on your phone
• App lock with fingerprint or face
• Encrypted backups you control, saved wherever you choose
• A built-in Vault for passwords and account details, encrypted on the device
• No analytics, no ads, no tracking

Arth is a tracking and planning tool, not financial advice.
