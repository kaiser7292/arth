Tap the **microphone** and say what you spent. Arth picks out the amount, merchant and other details, asks about anything missing, and fills in the form for you.

## Add an expense by voice

1. **Home tab → mic icon** at the top.
2. Say the expense naturally, for example:
   - "Spent 450 at Swiggy for lunch"
   - "Groceries at DMart 1200"
   - "Paid 850 for electricity yesterday"
3. Arth asks follow-up questions for anything it couldn't work out - **category**, **how you paid** (UPI, cash or card) and **which account** (say the bank name or last 4 digits). Say **skip** to leave any of them blank.
4. Arth opens the expense form, already filled in. Check it and tap **Save**.

Arth matches what you say against your own categories, accounts, payment modes and Hisaab people, so "HDFC" or "4521" finds your account. "Yesterday" and "day before yesterday" set the date; otherwise it's today.

**Split it:** add "split with" and a name - "Dinner at Barbeque Nation 3000, split with Rahul" - and the split is filled in too.

## Record a transfer by voice

Say the word **transfer**, for example "Transfer 5000 from HDFC to ICICI". Arth asks for anything missing (amount, from, to), reads the transfer back, and saves it when you say **yes**. Say **no** to cancel.

## Search by voice

On the **Transactions** tab, tap the mic in the search bar and say what you're looking for. With [Natural Language Search](ai-assistant) on, phrases like "coffee last week" work too, and the search runs as soon as you stop speaking.

## Voice settings

**Settings tab → Automation → Voice Input.**

- **Voice Sound** - pick the voice Arth speaks with. Tap **Preview** to hear each one.
- **Behaviour** - turn speak-back on, so Arth reads its follow-up questions aloud, or off for silent mode, where questions only appear as text.

## Common situations

**"It keeps asking which account."** More than one account matched what you said. Say the last 4 digits to pick one.

**"It didn't catch the amount."** Say the number clearly near the start - "450 at Swiggy" works better than a long sentence. You can always correct the form before saving.

**"The mic does nothing."** Allow Arth's microphone permission in Android settings. Speech recognition uses your phone's built-in speech service.
