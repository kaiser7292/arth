The **Transactions** tab (second tab at the bottom) is your full ledger. Everything you've approved or logged is here.

## The five pages

Swipe left or right, or tap the labels at the top:

- **All** - everything together.
- **Expenses** - money spent.
- **Committed** - forecasts and upcoming dues you expect to pay.
- **Credits** - salary, refunds and other money in.
- **Transfers** - money moved between your own accounts. The top of the list shows the count and total for your current filters.

Each **date heading** shows that day's net total, so you can see what a day cost you at a glance. On **All**, credits and debits are each counted the right way.

## Add a transaction

Tap **+** (bottom-right) and pick **Add Expense**, **Add Credit** or **Add Transfer**. They all open the same form, with a switch at the top:

- **Spent** - who you paid, the category (Arth suggests one as you type), and which account it was paid from.
- **Received** - who sent it, what it is (Salary, Interest, Refund, Cashback, Reimbursement, Gift or Other), a category (the same categories as spending) and which account it went into. Picking **Refund** lets you choose the expense it refunds.
- **Transfer** - the two accounts.

Notes, payment mode, splits, extra payment sources and tags are under **More options**. The form starts on the account you used last time. When editing something you entered yourself, you can switch it between Spent and Received; transactions from bank SMS keep the type the bank sent.

You can also add an expense by talking - see [Voice input](voice-input).

## Search

Type in the search box to match merchant, description and more. Tap the **mic** to search by voice.

With **Natural Language Search** turned on (Settings tab → Automation → Arth AI), you can type things like "food last month" or "coffee last week". See [AI assistant](ai-assistant).

## Filter

Tap the **funnel** next to the search box. You can filter by:

- Date range
- **Category**, **Account**, **Payment Mode**, **Merchant** and **Tags**
- **Status** - All, **Pending Review** or **Approved**
- **Avoidability** - unavoidable or avoidable
- **Refund Status** - refunded or not refunded
- **Smart Rule** - transactions a particular rule touched

The funnel is filled in while filters are active, and each filter appears as a chip under the search box - tap its ✕ to remove it.

### Saved views

Save a combination of filters you use often as a named **view**, then switch to it with one tap from the **Saved Views** row. You can set one as your default, or delete a view you no longer need.

## Sort

Tap the **sort** icon (two arrows) in the header:

- Date (newest first) - the default
- Date (oldest first)
- Amount (highest first)
- Amount (lowest first)
- Alphabetical (A-Z)

The icon is highlighted when you're not on the default. Arth remembers your choice. Sorting is also available when you drill into a Budget category or an Insights list.

## Edit many at once

Long-press a transaction to start selecting, then tap more. The bar at the bottom lets you **Set Category**, **Set Account**, **Set Payment Mode** or **Set Merchant** for all of them together.

## Open a transaction

Tap any row to see its details. From there you can edit it, split it with someone, add tags, set a reminder, apply a Smart Rule (the ⚡ icon), or mark it as a transfer, fixed deposit, investment or loan payment.

## Delete and undo

Delete a transaction from its detail screen or the list. A message appears with **Undo** - tap it to bring the transaction straight back. After that it's still in the [Recycle Bin](recycle-bin) for 30 days.

Split expenses don't offer Undo (restoring one wouldn't bring back its Hisaab entry). You're asked to confirm instead.
