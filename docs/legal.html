Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Arth is built on a simple principle: your financial data belongs to you, stored only on your device. These documents explain what that means in practice.

Privacy Policy
Effective: 20 September 2026

What we collect

Nothing. Arth does not collect, transmit, or store any personal or financial data on any server. All data — transactions, accounts, SMS logs, preferences — lives exclusively in a SQLite database on your Android device.

SMS access

Arth requests READ_SMS permission to detect bank transactions. Processing happens entirely on-device. No SMS content is ever transmitted externally. Only messages from recognised bank senders are read.

Internet access

Internet permission is used only for features you explicitly enable:

  • Connecting to Zerodha Kite, Angel One, or Zebpay via their official APIs — credentials go directly to those services, not through Arth's servers (there are none)
  • Google Drive backup, if enabled: your encrypted backup file goes directly to your own Drive account

When these features are not in use, Arth makes no network requests.

Backups

The backup feature creates an AES-GCM encrypted file on your device. If you enable Google Drive backup, this encrypted file is uploaded directly to your personal Drive. We have no access to it.

Analytics and tracking

There are no analytics SDKs, crash reporters, or tracking libraries in Arth. We have zero visibility into how you use the app.

Children

Arth is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be published here with a new effective date. Continued use of the app after that date constitutes acceptance.
