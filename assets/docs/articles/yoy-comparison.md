The **Year-over-Year comparison** (Goals tab → Analyse section → Year-over-Year) puts this fiscal year and the previous one side by side so you can see at a glance whether your finances are moving in the right direction.

## What is shown

Each category appears as a row with two columns — the previous FY total on the left and the current FY total on the right. A directional indicator shows whether the change is favourable or not.

**Expenses** — total spending across all categories. Lower is better; a year-on-year increase here is a warning that lifestyle costs are rising faster than income.

**Savings Snapshot** — actual savings (credits to savings accounts minus expenses) for the FY. Higher is better.

**Investment Buckets** — total contributions across all your investment buckets (SIPs, direct equity, mutual funds, etc. that you track in Arth). Higher is better.

**Milestones** — total contributions toward life milestones (down payment, education fund, emergency fund, etc.). Higher is better.

**Loans and Debts** — total EMIs paid plus prepayments for the FY, expressed in INR only. The direction is nuanced: the absolute outflow going lower year-over-year is good (debt reducing), but paying more than planned within a year is also good (faster payoff). Reading the number in context matters — a higher outflow caused by a large prepayment is positive, while a higher outflow caused by taking on a new loan may need attention.

## Reading the comparison

Arth shows only INR amounts. Non-INR loans and investments are excluded from the comparison totals. If you have investments tracked in foreign currency, their INR equivalent at the time of contribution is not currently reflected here.

For the current FY, the numbers represent what has happened so far — the year is not over yet, so the current-FY column will be lower than the previous one until the FY ends, even if you are on pace.

## Navigating fiscal years

The comparison always shows the most recently completed FY against the current FY. You cannot currently compare two past FYs against each other — that is a single fixed view.

## Common situations

**"My current FY investment total looks much lower than last year."** The current FY is probably not yet complete. The column shows what has actually been contributed so far, not the projected total. Check the Yearly Plan for a projection.

**"My loan outflow went up a lot this year."** If you made a large prepayment, that is captured here alongside EMIs. A higher outflow from prepayments means you are reducing debt faster — check the loan detail screen to confirm.

**"I can't see data for a category."** If you had no activity in a category in one or both years, that row may show zero or be omitted. For example, if you only started tracking milestones this FY, last year's column will be zero.
