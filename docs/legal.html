Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Arth is built on a simple principle: your financial data belongs to you, stored only on your device. These documents explain what that means in practice.

Privacy Policy
Effective: 26 September 2026

This policy covers Arth, the Android app published by Sourav Baid (“we”). Arth has no user accounts and no Arth cloud. Your financial records are stored in a database on your phone, and we cannot see them.

Data stored on your device

Transactions, accounts, balances, budgets, loans, investments, family ledger (Hisaab) entries, reminders, settings and any passwords you save in the Vault are stored only on your device. Vault entries and broker credentials are encrypted with a key protected by Android’s secure keystore. None of this is sent to us.

SMS messages

If you allow it, Arth reads the SMS messages on your phone to find bank, card and UPI transaction alerts. It keeps only messages from banks and payment services and ignores personal messages. From those alerts it extracts the amount, date, merchant, account or card last 4 digits and balance, and uses them to add your transactions and keep account balances up to date. Recognised alerts are stored on your device so you can review them. SMS messages are processed only on your device and are never uploaded, shared or sent to any server, including ours. You can turn SMS reading off in Settings, and revoke the permission in Android settings, at any time.

Features that use the internet

Arth only goes online for the features below, and only when you use them. Broker connections use your own API credentials (“bring your own key”), created in your broker’s developer console; your use of them is governed by your broker’s terms, and Arth is not affiliated with any broker. Broker connections are read-only: Arth never places orders or moves money.

  • Zerodha Kite (Kite Connect terms, Zerodha privacy policy). Your Kite API key is stored encrypted on your device. When you connect Kite, you sign in on Zerodha’s own login page. Zerodha returns a one-time request token, which Arth sends to our connection server (integration.souravbaid.com) to exchange for an access token, because Zerodha requires that step to run on a registered server. The server passes the access token back to your phone and does not store it or any of your data. After that, Arth fetches your holdings and positions directly from Zerodha.
  • Angel One (terms, privacy policy) and Zebpay (legal & privacy). The API keys, secrets, client ID, PIN and 2FA (TOTP) secret you enter are sent directly to Angel One or Zebpay to fetch your holdings. They are stored only on your device, encrypted, so Arth can refresh your session, and never pass through our servers.
  • Vault autofill for Kite. If you link a Vault entry to Kite, Arth fills your Zerodha user ID, password and 2FA code into Zerodha’s login page on your device. They go only to Zerodha.
  • Arth AI. The first time you use the assistant, Arth downloads a language model file from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). Like any download, this reveals your IP address to Hugging Face. The assistant then runs entirely on your device; your questions and data are never sent anywhere.
  • Voice entry. Voice entry uses your phone’s speech recognition service (usually Google). Depending on your phone’s settings, that service may process your voice on its own servers under its own privacy policy. Arth receives only the recognised text.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy, which applies to the data you send them.

Other permissions

  • Calendar, only if you turn on calendar sync, to add bills, EMIs and reminders to the calendar you choose.
  • Microphone, only while you use voice entry.
  • Notifications, alarms and start on boot, for reminders and the daily summary.
  • Fingerprint or face, for app lock. Biometric data is handled by Android and never seen by Arth.

Backups

Backups are encrypted files (AES-GCM) saved on your device or wherever you choose to put them, such as your Downloads folder or a cloud drive you share them to. We never receive them.

Analytics, ads and tracking

Arth contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking libraries. We do not sell or share your data, and we have no visibility into how you use the app.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual records in the app (deleted items are kept in the Recycle Bin for 30 days), disconnect any broker in Settings, or remove everything by clearing Arth’s storage or uninstalling the app. Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.
