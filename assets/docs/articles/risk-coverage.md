**Risk Coverage** keeps all your insurance policies in one place and checks whether your cover is enough for your income and family.

## Where to find it

**Goals tab → Track → Risk Coverage.** The row shows how many policies you have and how many gaps Arth found (or "All covered").

## Add a policy

1. Tap **Add policy**.
2. Pick the type: **Term**, **Health**, **Life / Endowment**, **Car**, **Home** or **Other**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Provider Name** (e.g. HDFC Life, Star Health) and **Policy Number** (optional)
   - **Sum Insured**
   - **Annual Premium** and how often you pay it (Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-annual or Annual)
   - **Start Date** and **Expiry Date**
   - For health policies, **Family Members Covered**
   - **Notes** (optional)
4. Tap **Add Policy**.

Tap a policy to edit it, or delete it from the edit screen. When a policy ends, update its details or delete it so the checks below stay accurate.

## Is my cover enough?

The summary card at the top shows your total annual premium and a quick check for each kind of cover:

- **Term** - your total term cover should be at least **10× your annual income**. Arth uses the in-hand income from your [Income Calculator](salary-calculator) for the current financial year, so set that up first.
- **Health** - at least **₹10 lakh per family member** covered.
- **Car** - any active car policy counts as covered.

Home insurance is tracked but not scored. Anything that falls short, or is missing, is listed as a **gap**, for example "Term cover 6× income - recommended 10×" or "No health insurance".

These are simple rules of thumb, not advice. Your own needs may be different.

## Where else it's used

The **Retirement readiness** report (Insights → Reports) uses your real policies. Adequate health cover adds to your readiness score. See [Reports](reports).
