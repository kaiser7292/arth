The **Financial Health grade** is a letter grade (A+ down to F) that summarises how well your actual financial behaviour matches your plan for the year. Tap the **Grade pill** on the Goals tab → Financial Health card to open the full breakdown.

## The score

Arth computes a score from 0 to 100 by evaluating five factors. Each factor has a weight:

| Factor | Weight | What it measures |
|--------|--------|-----------------|
| **Savings** | 30% | Whether your actual savings rate is meeting your target |
| **Investments** | 25% | Whether investment bucket contributions are on pace |
| **Spending** | 20% | Whether expenses are within your planned budget |
| **Goals** | 15% | Progress on life milestones and bucket targets |
| **Surplus** | 10% | Whether there is meaningful breathing room each month |

The weighted total gives a score from 0–100. Each factor also shows its own mini score and a one-line explanation of why it scored that way.

## Grade thresholds

| Grade | Score | What it means |
|-------|-------|---------------|
| **A+** | 90–100 | Excellent — plan is firing on all cylinders |
| **A** | 80–89 | Great — strong across the board, minor gaps only |
| **B** | 70–79 | Good — solid foundation with a few areas to improve |
| **C** | 55–69 | Needs attention — material gaps in one or more areas |
| **D** | 40–54 | Critical — significant gaps, act now |
| **F** | 0–39 | Off-plan — major correction needed urgently |

## How to improve your grade

The grade detail screen shows the two weakest factors and a specific action for each. Common paths:

- **Low Savings score** — increase contributions to your savings account or reduce discretionary spending so more income is retained.
- **Low Investments score** — catch up on investment bucket contributions; even small additions each month compound quickly.
- **Low Spending score** — you're over-budget in one or more categories; check the Budget tab for the biggest overruns.
- **Low Goals score** — you have milestones or buckets that haven't received contributions this FY; even a small catch-up improves the score.
- **Low Surplus score** — your fixed commitments (EMIs, bucket targets, milestones) are consuming most of your income; review whether any targets can shift.

## Monthly Headroom

The **Monthly Headroom** number on the Financial Health card is not the same as your savings rate. It is the theoretical surplus per month after all planned commitments (target savings, investment buckets, milestone contributions, loan EMIs) are subtracted from your monthly income.

- **Positive** — you have room for one-off expenses or extra savings.
- **Zero / amber** — income is fully committed; any unexpected cost creates a shortfall.
- **Negative / red** — your planned commitments exceed your income. Either income is understated or targets need adjusting.

## On-track signal

Below the progress bar, Arth shows one of:

- **On track · projected X% by [month year]** — at your current pace, you will meet your savings rate target by year-end.
- **₹X/mo to reach Y%** — you are behind; this is the extra monthly saving needed to close the gap by fiscal year-end.

This signal only appears when you have set a savings rate target (Goals → Income Calculator).

## Where the grade lives

- **Goals tab → Financial Health card** — grade pill, headroom, savings rate, progress bar, on-track signal.
- **Tap the grade pill** → Grade detail screen with factor breakdown and improvement tips.
- The grade refreshes whenever your transactions are updated (it is not computed live; it updates when the Goals tab loads).
