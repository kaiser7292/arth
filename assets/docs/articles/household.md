A household lets you and up to 4 others (a partner, parents, siblings) see shared spending together. Each person keeps their own Arth and their own data, and chooses what to share.

## Start one

Settings → Household → **Start a household**. Give it a name and say how the others will see you. You're the one who invites and approves people.

## Invite someone

On the next screen Arth shows a QR code. On their phone: Settings → Household → **Join a household**, then scan it. Not together? Tap **Send an invite link instead** and send it on WhatsApp; they open it on their phone. An invite works once and for 24 hours.

## Check codes

Both phones show a 6-digit check code while someone joins. Approve only if the two match. If you're apart, read it out on a call. Matching codes mean nobody tampered with the invite on the way.

## Choose what to share

Nothing is shared just by joining. Under **What you share**:

- **Household categories in full** - each approved spend in a household category (Groceries, Rent, Utilities, Household help, Fuel), with the amount, your share and the merchant.
- **Totals of everything else** - one total a month per category. No transactions, no merchants.
- **Every transaction** - all approved spends, like a joint account.
- **Savings rate** - a percentage each month, never your salary.
- **Account balances** - only the accounts you pick.

The screen shows a preview of what the others will see this month. Pending items, income, message text, notes, account numbers, the Vault, loans and investments are never shared.

Under **Household categories** you choose which of your own categories count as which household category. Arth matches most by name ("Grocery" → Groceries).

## Keeping in sync

Arth syncs when you open it, a few seconds after you change something, and every hour or so in the background on Android. On iPhone it syncs when Arth opens. Pull down on the Household screen to sync now.

## Privacy

Everything you share is locked on your phone with a key only your household's phones have, before it leaves. It travels through Arth's relay, which holds locked envelopes it can't open and deletes them once every phone has them (at most 90 days). The relay never sees names, amounts, categories or merchants.

## Leave or remove

- **Leave household**: what you shared disappears from the others' phones, and what they shared disappears from yours. Your own data stays.
- Whoever started the household can **remove** someone, which also changes the household's key so their phone can't read anything new. Closing the household ends it for everyone.

## New phone

Your encrypted backup includes your household and its keys. Restore it on the new phone and you carry on as yourself; the old phone stops syncing. A scheduled backup doesn't include the keys, so after restoring one, leave the household on that phone and ask to be invited again.
