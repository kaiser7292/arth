**Check-ins** are short reviews you swipe through, one card at a time, like [Catch Up](catch-up). Each one looks at a different part of your money and asks a quick question. Swipe right for the main action, left to skip.

## Where to find them

**Home tab → Check-ins card.** It only appears when a check-in has something for you, and it shows how many cards each one has. Tap a check-in to start it.

When you finish one, Arth moves you on to the next check-in that has cards. After the last one you see **You're all caught up**, with a summary and a **Back to Home** button.

To hide the card, go to Settings tab → Preferences & Security → Home Cards → **Check-ins**.

## Month-end check

One card per bank account, credit card and wallet. Each compares **Arth's balance** with the **last balance your bank sent by SMS**.

- **Matches your bank** - swipe **Looks right** to confirm it.
- **Off by ₹X** - something may be missing or approved twice. Tap **Open ledger** to find it. See [Reconciling a ledger](reconciliation).
- If you've had transactions since the bank's last balance SMS, or there's no balance SMS at all, Arth can't compare exactly and tells you to check your bank app.

The Home card only offers the month-end check from the **28th of the month to the 7th of the next**. Each month starts fresh.

## Settle up

One card per person who owes you money in [Hisaab](hisaab), showing what they owe and when you last had an entry.

- **Remind** - opens your phone's share sheet (WhatsApp, SMS or anything else) with a ready-written message.
- **Mark settled** - records a settlement for the full balance.
- **Open ledger** - opens that person's Hisaab.

Someone you reminded in the last 7 days isn't counted on the Home card again, so you aren't nagged.

## Subscriptions

One card per recurring payment Arth has spotted. A card appears when:

- it's a **new subscription** you haven't reviewed yet,
- you marked it to cancel but it's **still charging you**, or
- you kept it, but haven't looked at it in **3 months** ("Still worth it?").

Each card shows the amount, how often it charges, the yearly cost, and when it last charged.

- **Keep** - it stays on your list.
- **Cancel this** - a note to yourself to cancel it. Arth flags it if it charges again.
- **Not a subscription** - removes it from your subscriptions.

## Monthly bills

Arth looks at the last six months of spending for payments that come at a steady rhythm - rent, a subscription, school fees - and asks **Is this a regular bill?** with the amount, how often and the usual day.

- **Yes, it's a bill** - it counts as an expected bill in the Budget tab's month-end projection until it's paid that month.
- **No, it varies** - it's treated as everyday spending from now on.

Arth refreshes these patterns once a day. A bill that stops (not seen for about one and a half times its usual gap) drops out on its own; one you confirmed is paused until it comes back.

## Rule suggestions

"You've filed Swiggy as Food 7 times. Make it automatic?"

When you've filed the same merchant under the same category **at least 4 times** (and at least 80% of the time), with no [Smart Rule](smart-rules) for it yet, Arth suggests one. Merchants that Arth's built-in categorisation already files correctly are left out, because a rule wouldn't change anything.

- **Create rule** - makes the Smart Rule for you.
- **Don't suggest again** - never shows this merchant again.

## Skipping and snoozing

If you finish a check-in having **skipped every card**, Arth takes that as "not now" and hides that check-in for **a week**. A message tells you when this happens. Acting on even one card doesn't snooze it.

## Undo

Like Catch Up, every action waits a few seconds before it's saved - tap **Undo** to take it back.
