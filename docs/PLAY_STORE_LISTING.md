## Full description (max 4,000)

Arth is an SMS-based money manager that brings your financial life into one private app. It reads the transaction alerts your bank already sends you, on your phone, and turns them into accounts, budgets, loans, goals and net worth that stay up to date by themselves. No bank login, no account to create, no cloud, no ads.

SEE IT ALL IN ONE PLACE
• Savings accounts, credit cards, wallets, deposits, loans and EPF in one place, with month-by-month ledgers
• Net worth and a balance sheet of everything you own and owe
• A financial health grade that shows what is strong and what needs work
• Year-over-year comparisons for your financial year (April to March)

PLAN AHEAD
• A yearly financial plan built from your income, expenses, investments and goals
• Income calculator for your in-hand salary, tax and EPF
• Life milestones and investment buckets with progress against target
• Loan schedules with prepayment planning, and retirement readiness
• A cash-flow simulator to test a decision before you make it

SPEND WITH INTENT
• Every bank, card and UPI alert becomes a transaction in a review queue. You approve it and it lands in the right account and budget
• Works with major Indian banks, credit cards and UPI apps; teach Arth any new SMS format once
• Smart rules categorise repeat merchants for you
• Monthly budgets by category, upcoming dues and reminders
• Insights that flag overspending, rising costs and small leaks, plus your spending personality
• Add cash spends and anything SMS misses by hand or by voice

MONEY WITH PEOPLE
• Hisaab, a family ledger for money lent and borrowed, where splitting an expense records each person's share automatically

WHY ARTH ASKS FOR SMS PERMISSION
Reading bank SMS is how Arth tracks your money without asking for your bank login. With your permission, Arth reads transaction SMS from banks, card issuers, UPI apps and EPFO to:
• record your spending, income and refunds
• keep account balances and credit card limits up to date
• track card dues and your EPF balance
Arth only looks at messages from banks and payment services, so personal chats are skipped, and bank messages such as OTPs are never turned into transactions. SMS are processed only on your phone and are never uploaded, shared or sent to any server. You can turn SMS reading off at any time in Settings, and still use Arth by adding transactions yourself.

INVESTMENTS (OPTIONAL)
Connect your own Zerodha Kite, Angel One or Zebpay API key to see holdings alongside everything else. Read-only: Arth never places orders or moves money. Arth is not affiliated with any broker.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No sign-up and no Arth servers: your data lives in a database on your phone
• App lock with fingerprint or face, and an encrypted Vault for logins
• Encrypted backups you control, saved wherever you choose
• No analytics, no ads, no tracking

Arth is a planning and tracking tool, not financial advice.
