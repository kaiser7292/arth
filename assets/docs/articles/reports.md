Arth can build in-depth reports from your own data. Each one runs on your phone and can be saved as a PDF.

## Where to find them

Each report lives where it belongs:

- **Financial health** - tap the **grade** on the Financial health card at the top of the Goals tab.
- **Retirement readiness** - the **Retirement readiness** tile under Plan on the Goals tab. You can switch it off in **Settings → Customise Arth**.
- **Loan payoff** - **Goals → Loans & debt → Payoff plan**.
- **Spending personality** - a slide in **Year in review** (Home → Insights → Explore → Year in review).

## The reports

### Financial health

Needs no input. Covers your net worth, savings rate, debt-to-income, emergency buffer (fixed deposits count towards it) and monthly EMI, led by the same grade as the Goals tab: your yearly plan's grade, with its factors (savings, investments, spending, goals, headroom). Tap the grade for how it's worked out - see [Financial Health grade explained](financial-health-grade). Before you have a yearly plan, the report grades savings rate, emergency fund, debt, spending discipline and diversification instead.

### Retirement readiness

Asks a few questions - your age, when you want to retire, monthly expenses today (excluding EMIs), and the returns you expect before and after retirement. It then works out:

- the **corpus** you'd need and how far along you are,
- the **monthly SIP** needed to fill the gap,
- conservative, moderate and optimistic scenarios, a phase-by-phase plan, and the risks to watch.

It uses your [insurance policies](risk-coverage) too - adequate health cover improves your readiness score.

### Loan payoff strategy

Compares paying off your loans **avalanche** style (highest interest first) against **snowball** (smallest balance first), with and without extra payments. You see the interest saved, the payoff timeline and the best order to clear your loans. Needs at least one active loan - see [Loans and EMI tracking](loans).

### Spending personality

Needs no input. Gives your spending an archetype from recent months, with its traits. It's one slide in Year in review.

## Save as PDF

Tap **Download PDF** at the bottom of the Financial health, Retirement readiness or Payoff plan screen. Arth creates the PDF on your phone and opens the share sheet so you can save or send it. Your answers to the retirement and loan questions are remembered for next time.

> A PDF contains your financial details. Share it only with people you trust.

These reports are estimates based on the numbers you've recorded and the assumptions you enter. They are not financial advice.
