**Catch Up** turns your [review queue](review-queue) into a deck of cards. You deal with one item at a time: swipe right to act on it, swipe left to skip. It's the fastest way to clear a backlog on your phone.

## Where to find it

- **Home tab → Review Queue card → Catch up (N).**
- **Home tab → Queue page → Catch up (N).**
- The **home screen widget**, which shows how many items are waiting ("N to catch up"). See [Notifications and the home screen widget](notifications).

## What's in the deck

Cards come in this order:

1. **Possible duplicates** - two or more rows that look like the same payment. Handled first, so you don't approve both copies one by one.
2. **Forecast matches** - a bank SMS that looks like a payment you were expecting.
3. **New bank transactions** from SMS, oldest first.
4. **Transactions that need a category.**

Upcoming and overdue dues aren't in the deck - they stay on the Review Queue and Home.

## Acting on a card

The card tells you what swiping right will do:

- **New transaction** → **Approve**. Arth suggests a category; tap it to change. Swipe left to skip, or tap **Reject**.
- **Needs a category** → **Pick category** (or **Save** if one is already suggested).
- **Matches a forecast** → **Same payment** marks the forecast as paid using the SMS. **Different payments** keeps both.
- **Possible duplicate** → **Keep newest** rejects the older copies. **Not duplicates** keeps them all.

Tap **Add description** on the card to note what the payment was for - it's saved straight away, even if you skip the card. Tap **Show original SMS** to read the bank message, or **Edit or split** to open the full transaction.

When you pick a category yourself, Arth remembers it for that merchant next time.

## "Same again?"

After you approve or file a card, Arth checks whether more cards in the deck are from the **same merchant**. If so, it asks whether to do the same to all of them:

- **Yes, all** - applies the same action and category to every one.
- **One by one** - carries on through the deck normally.

## Undo

Every action waits **a few seconds** before it's saved. Tap **Undo** on the message at the bottom and nothing is written. If the app closes during that time, the item just stays in the queue.

## When you're done

The deck ends on **You're all caught up**, with a summary of what you approved, rejected, sorted out and skipped. Skipped items are still in the Review Queue for next time.
