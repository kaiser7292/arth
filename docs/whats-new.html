What's new

Better every week.

Arth updates often. Here's what each update changed, newest first. Updates arrive through Google Play and keep all your data.

Arth 4.28.2
29 Oct 2026
LATEST

One split, and whoever paid

  • One split, in one place: split a bill with your household from the transaction's own Split, and remove it there; it stays removed. Arth no longer splits anything by itself. Shared with Home is now one switch: whether the household sees a transaction.
  • Paid with someone else's card: record that your partner paid, and their phone finds the matching transaction on their card (or asks which account) and splits it, so their card, budget and Hisaab all agree.
  • Details in Hisaab: entries from your household show the merchant and category, not just an amount.
  • Together, tidier: one Filter for who paid, who owes whom and category; long pickers like category mapping scroll and have search. Monthly totals of personal spending are gone.
  • New versions (Android): Arth tells you when an update is out, from the Play Store or GitHub, wherever you installed it from. Turn it off in Settings.
  • Back on every screen: the Hisaab ledger keeps its back and Home buttons on iPhone.
Arth 4.28.1
29 Oct 2026

Clearer about what's shared

  • Privacy, in plain words: the welcome screen and Help now explain households: nothing is shared unless you join one, and what you share is locked so only your household can read it.
Arth 4.28
29 Oct 2026

Money, together

  • Households: share spending with your partner or family, up to 5 people. Invite with a QR code or a link; each of you keeps your own Arth and chooses what to share.
  • Bills split themselves: rent, groceries and other household spends are shared and split as you approve them. Mark any transaction shared or personal, split equally, pay it all, or set your own share.
  • Together: what you spent together this month, by person and by category, right from Home. Tap any total for its transactions, and filter by who, what or who owes whom.
  • Their categories, your way: when someone files a bill under a category you don't have, map it to one of yours or create it.
  • Hisaab on both phones: your share of their bills lands in your Hisaab and your budget by itself, settle-ups show on both sides, and Arth tells you when both phones agree.
  • Plan together: agree a household budget, and save towards shared goals from your own investment buckets.
  • Private by design: what you share is locked on your phone before it leaves; Arth's relay can't read it and deletes it once everyone has it.
Arth 4.27
28 Oct 2026

Arth on your home screen

  • Three new widgets: what's left of this month's budget and whether you're on pace, how many transactions are waiting, and the newest ones to review.
  • Approve from the home screen: tick or cross new transactions on the large widget without opening Arth.
  • Add in one tap: an expense, by voice, or from a picture, straight from the widget.
  • Private when you want: with Hide amounts on, the widgets hide amounts too, and with the app lock on, approving waits until you unlock.
Arth 4.26.2
27 Oct 2026

Behind the scenes

  • Updated build tools and fixes that let Arth be built for iPhone with the latest Xcode. Nothing changes in how Arth works on Android.
Arth 4.26.1
27 Oct 2026

Dates read right

  • Dates with the month written out, like "24 October 2026" on PhonePe receipts, are read from pictures now, and you can link them to Date / time.
  • Tab names fit: "Transactions" no longer gets cut short on smaller screens.
  • No false alarm: Home no longer says "Over Budget" before you've set a budget.
Arth 4.26
27 Oct 2026

Budget numbers you can follow

  • See how a total adds up: Budget lists and the monthly summary show what you paid, others' share of split bills, refunds and what counts as yours. Split payments say what your share was.
  • Refunds count everywhere: tap a category on the Budget tab and its total and list take off linked refunds, the same as the bar does.
  • Spending Split, redrawn: unavoidable, discretionary and "not classified yet". Tap a group for its categories, a category for its transactions, and Select to mark many at once.
  • Flexible spend replaces the unavoidable ratio in the monthly summary: what you could cut back on, against last month.
  • New filters: find transactions with no category, payment mode, account, tags or merchant, or not classified yet.
  • Catch Up totals add up: approvals made on the edit screen count, and split bills show your share of what was paid.
  • Fixed: "Database Init Failed" and "Couldn't save expense" errors, sharing a payment screenshot to Arth while it's open, and investment buckets that wouldn't delete.
Arth 4.25
23 Oct 2026

Pictures read more accurately

  • The ₹ sign is read correctly: amounts no longer come out with an extra 7 in front.
  • Pick just the words you need: when a shop name and the amount share a line, tap the words that make each field.

Arth 4.24
23 Oct 2026

Point out what a picture says

  • Link the text to fields: if Arth misses something in a payment picture, tap the text on the picture and say what it is. It fills in the transaction straight away.
  • Save it as a template: tick one box and Arth reads pictures like it the same way from then on.
  • Edit your templates: in Settings, add a template, rename one, change its fields or teach it again with a new picture.
  • Fixed: reading a picture could fail with a database error on some phones.
Arth 4.23.1
23 Oct 2026

Arth on tablets

  • Wi-Fi tablets welcome: Arth now installs on tablets without a SIM, like the Galaxy Tab. Everything works except reading SMS, since there are none; add transactions by hand or from a picture.
  • No camera needed: phones and tablets without a camera can install Arth too, and pick pictures from the gallery.
Arth 4.23
23 Oct 2026

Add a transaction from a picture

  • From a picture: share a payment screenshot to Arth, pick one from your gallery, or take a photo of a receipt. Arth reads the amount, who was paid, the date and the reference on your phone, and fills in the transaction for you to check.
  • Your UPI IDs pick the account: add your UPI IDs to an account, and a payment showing one of them goes to that account by itself.
  • Teach Arth a layout: if something is missed, tap it on the picture. Arth reads pictures like it the same way from then on.

Arth 4.21
20 Oct 2026

Your own money moving is no longer spending

  • Real transfers: paying your credit card bill or moving money to another of your accounts now counts as a transfer, not spending, and old ones can be fixed in one go.
  • Watch the setup happen: reading your messages and applying your AI assistant's setup show their progress, and applying the setup is much faster.
  • Your accounts, your call: during setup, see where each account Arth found came from and switch off any you don't want tracked.
  • Also: new categories get icons that fit, loan accounts are recognised as loans, and connecting Zerodha, Angel One or Zebpay can create the account for you.
