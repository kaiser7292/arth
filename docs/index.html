What Arth does

Everything in one place, nowhere else.

Most finance apps want your data. Arth keeps it where it belongs: on your device, under your control.

Expense Tracking
Log manually or let Arth read your bank SMS and auto-create entries. Review and approve each one before it lands.
Budget Management
Category-level budgets with monthly tracking, pie charts, and a monthly summary. See exactly where money goes.
All Account Types
Savings, credit cards, wallets, demat, PPF/EPF/NPS pension, and loans. One unified ledger with opening balances.
Goals & Net Worth
Investment buckets, life milestones, balance sheet, and year-on-year comparisons for Indian FY (Apr-Mar).
Loan Engine
Full amortisation with prepayment. Choose reduce-tenure or reduce-EMI and see the exact impact instantly.
Family Ledger (Hisaab)
Track money lent or borrowed from family and friends. Running balances, settlements, full history.
Inside the app

Built for people who actually check.

A full financial year of budgets, every account in a single ledger, and a review queue that waits for your approval. The detail is always there when you want it.

Power features

The details that make it yours.

Features built for people who want their finance tool to work exactly the way they think.

Smart
Smart SMS Templates
Your bank sends SMS in a format Arth doesn't recognise? Teach it. Build a template by tagging the amount, account, and merchant in a sample message. No coding required. Arth compiles it to a regex, normalises both the template and incoming SMS the same way, and starts detecting that format instantly. Includes a live test and diagnose mode to see exactly why a message matched or didn't.
Visual builder Live test Any bank Any format
Smart
Smart Rules
Stop re-categorising the same merchants every month. Set a rule like "merchant contains Swiggy = Food and Dining" once, and Arth applies it automatically to every new expense that matches. Rules stack in priority order, support substring and exact matching, and can set category, notes, or tags all at once.
Auto-categorise Priority rules Any merchant
Simulator
Cash Flow Simulator
Project your bank balance weeks or months forward before a big decision. Model a salary hike, a new EMI, an early loan prepayment, or a one-time expense, and see how your cash position holds up. What-if scenarios run entirely on your device, instantly.
What-if scenarios EMI modelling Salary changes
On-device AI
AI Financial Assistant
Ask natural language questions about your finances: "how much did I spend on food last month?", "what's my savings rate this year?", "show me my biggest expenses". Powered by an on-device language model (no cloud, no API calls). Your data never leaves the phone, not even for AI.
On-device model No cloud Natural language
Privacy first

Zero cloud. Zero tracking. Zero compromise.

Arth runs on a SQLite database that lives only on your device, protected by Android's OS-level security. Nothing is ever uploaded, nothing is ever shared.

0

0
Third-party analytics, trackers, or crash reporters
100%
Offline. Works without internet, always, by design
Auto-detection

Your bank messages, understood automatically.

Arth reads your SMS inbox and recognises transactions from 25+ Indian banks. Debits, credits, and transfers are parsed on-device and queued for your review. You approve, edit, or reject each entry before it gets recorded.

✓Build custom templates for any SMS format
✓Review queue. Nothing gets added without your approval
