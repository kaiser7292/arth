The **Financial Health grade** is a letter grade — A+ down to F — that summarises how well your actual financial behaviour matches your plan for the year. You'll find it as a small pill on the Financial Health card in the Goals tab. Tap the pill to open the full grade breakdown.

## How the score works

Arth computes a score from 0 to 100 by evaluating five factors. Each factor contributes a weighted portion to the total:

- **Savings (30%)** — whether your actual savings rate is meeting your target
- **Investments (25%)** — whether your investment bucket contributions are on pace for the year
- **Spending (20%)** — whether your expenses are within your planned budget
- **Goals (15%)** — your progress on life milestones and investment bucket targets
- **Surplus (10%)** — whether there is meaningful breathing room each month after all commitments

The weighted total becomes your score. Each factor also has its own mini score from 0–100, shown on the grade detail screen with a progress bar and a one-line explanation of why it scored that way.

## What the grades mean

- **A+** (90–100) — Excellent. Your plan is firing on all cylinders.
- **A** (80–89) — Great. Strong across the board with only minor gaps.
- **B** (70–79) — Good. Solid foundation but a few areas to improve.
- **C** (55–69) — Needs attention. Material gaps in one or more areas.
- **D** (40–54) — Critical. Significant gaps; act now to avoid shortfalls.
- **F** (0–39) — Off-plan. Major correction needed urgently.

## How to improve your grade

The grade detail screen shows the two weakest factors and a specific action for each. The common paths to a better grade:

**Low Savings score** — increase contributions to your savings account or reduce discretionary spending so more income is actually retained. Even a 2–3% improvement in savings rate moves the score noticeably.

**Low Investments score** — you are under-contributing to your investment buckets for the year. Catch up with additional contributions this month, or reduce a target that is no longer realistic.

**Low Spending score** — you are over-budget in one or more categories. Check the Budget tab for the biggest overruns and look at what is driving them.

**Low Goals score** — you have milestones or buckets that have received little or no contribution this FY. Even a small consistent catch-up each month improves this score.

**Low Surplus score** — your fixed commitments (EMIs, bucket targets, milestone contributions) are consuming most of your income. Review whether any target can shift or whether income is understated in the Income Calculator.

## Monthly Headroom

The **Monthly Headroom** number on the Financial Health card is different from your savings rate. It is the theoretical surplus each month after all planned commitments — target savings, investment buckets, milestone contributions, and loan EMIs — are subtracted from your monthly income.

A positive number means you have breathing room for one-off expenses or extra savings. When it shows amber, your income is fully committed and any unexpected cost creates a shortfall. When it shows red, your planned commitments exceed your income — either income is understated in the Income Calculator, or the combined targets are too aggressive.

## On-track signal

Below the savings progress bar, Arth shows one of two signals:

**On track · projected X% by [month year]** — at your current pace, you will meet your savings rate target by fiscal year-end.

**₹X/mo to reach Y% by [month year]** — you are running behind. This is the extra monthly saving you need to close the gap by year-end.

This signal only appears when you have set a savings rate target. You set that in the Income Calculator under Goals.

## Where to find the grade

The grade pill lives on the Goals tab inside the Financial Health card. Tap it to open the full detail screen showing your score, the five factors with individual bars and explanations, tips for the next grade up, and the full grade scale with your current position marked.

The grade refreshes each time the Goals tab loads — it is not a live counter, so adding a single expense will not change it mid-session.
