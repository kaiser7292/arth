What's new

Better every week.

Arth updates often. Here's what each update changed, newest first. Updates arrive through Google Play and keep all your data.

Arth 4.23.1
23 Oct 2026
LATEST

Arth on tablets

  • Wi-Fi tablets welcome: Arth now installs on tablets without a SIM, like the Galaxy Tab. Everything works except reading SMS, since there are none; add transactions by hand or from a picture.
  • No camera needed: phones and tablets without a camera can install Arth too, and pick pictures from the gallery.
Arth 4.23
23 Oct 2026

Add a transaction from a picture

  • From a picture: share a payment screenshot to Arth, pick one from your gallery, or take a photo of a receipt. Arth reads the amount, who was paid, the date and the reference on your phone, and fills in the transaction for you to check.
  • Your UPI IDs pick the account: add your UPI IDs to an account, and a payment showing one of them goes to that account by itself.
  • Teach Arth a layout: if something is missed, tap it on the picture. Arth reads pictures like it the same way from then on.
Arth 4.22.2
20 Oct 2026

Open your backups from the Files app

  • Arth now shows up under Open with for backups in the Files app, including the ones you already have.
Arth 4.22.1
20 Oct 2026

Open a backup straight in Arth

  • Open with Arth: tap an Arth backup in your files or downloads and choose Arth. It goes straight to restore, ready for your password.
  • No accounts missed during setup: Arth now finishes reading your messages before showing the accounts it found.
Arth 4.22
20 Oct 2026

Set up your way: with AI, or by hand

  • Pick your route: after Arth reads your messages, choose AI assisted or manual setup, and only that route's steps follow. No more being asked for budgets your AI already set.
  • Calmer progress screens: rows arrive one at a time and the numbers count up smoothly.
  • Ignore an account: stop Arth reading messages for any account, and track it again whenever you like.
Arth 4.21
20 Oct 2026

Your own money moving is no longer spending

  • Real transfers: paying your credit card bill or moving money to another of your accounts now counts as a transfer, not spending, and old ones can be fixed in one go.
  • Watch the setup happen: reading your messages and applying your AI assistant's setup show their progress, and applying the setup is much faster.
  • Your accounts, your call: during setup, see where each account Arth found came from and switch off any you don't want tracked.
  • Also: new categories get icons that fit, loan accounts are recognised as loans, and connecting Zerodha, Angel One or Zebpay can create the account for you.
Arth 4.20.2
19 Oct 2026

FD payouts and merging merchants

  • FD matured or closed: when Arth doesn't spot a fixed deposit paying out, mark the credit yourself and pick the FD. It stops counting as income.
  • Merging merchants is easier: search, see similar names first, and confirm before anything changes.
Arth 4.20.1
18 Oct 2026

Merchants, without the fiddly taps

  • Undo anything: renamed, merged or recategorised a merchant by mistake? Tap Undo. It stays on screen until you close it.
  • Bigger buttons on the merchant screens, with the reset options kept out of the way.
Arth 4.20
18 Oct 2026

Your Year in Review, now a story with music

  • A story, not a report: full-screen, colourful slides that play one after another. Tap to skip ahead or go back, hold to pause.
  • Everything moves: numbers count up, your savings fill a ring, your net worth draws itself, and your favourite merchants take the podium.
  • Music to go with it, made just for Arth. Turn it on with the speaker button.
  • Also: see at a glance which accounts are open or closed, and teaching Arth a card message is smoother.
Arth 4.19
18 Oct 2026

Teaching Arth a message, now mostly one tap

  • Arth guesses, you confirm: it works out the amount, merchant, date and account from similar messages and from what you taught it before. Fix just the part it got wrong.
  • Accounts stay right: a savings account no longer turns up as a credit card, and accounts saved twice can be merged.
  • Smarter AI suggestions that build on what you've already set up, and a quicker scan of your messages.
