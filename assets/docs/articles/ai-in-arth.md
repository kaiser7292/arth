Arth has two kinds of AI. Both live in **Settings → AI**, and each has the same name everywhere in the app.

## On this phone

The AI built into some newer phones: Gemini Nano on Android, Apple Intelligence on iPhone (iPhone 15 Pro or later, iOS 26). It works by itself and **nothing leaves the phone**.

- It reads the bank messages Arth's own patterns don't know.
- It also suggests categories, spots names that are one place, learns message formats and more. Each has its own switch.
- Everything it finds waits for you in **Catch Up** before it counts.

On phones without either, Settings → AI says so in one line. See **On-device AI (Gemini Nano, Apple Intelligence)** for the details.

## With your AI assistant

ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or any chat app. It can do more, because it sees your history, but **your data goes to that company** when you share the file.

- **Ask your AI assistant:** set up everything, fix your whole history, tidy categories, suggest a budget, find what's missing, or read unread messages. You share one file, paste the reply back, and check every change before it's applied.
- **Ask about my money:** a file to chat about. Nothing comes back into Arth.

Arth never sends anything by itself. You choose where the file goes. See **Ask your AI assistant and Export for AI Insights** for the steps.

## What's waiting for you

When the AI has something for you to decide, it's a card in **Catch Up**, next to your transactions:

- **Your assistant's reply is waiting:** you shared a file; paste the reply.
- **Learn a message format:** read this bank's format by itself from now on.
- **Same place?:** two merchant names that look like one place.

Home's **Action required** card counts them as "from AI to check".

## What AI changed

**Settings → AI → What AI changed** lists every set of changes applied from your assistant, and every bank balance match, with search, filters and sort. The latest set from your assistant can be undone until you apply the next one. Undo puts all your data back as it was just before.

## Data health

**Settings → Data health** is Arth's own checks, with no AI: duplicates, transfers, refunds left unlinked, balances that don't match the bank, and more. Each row shows what it found and how to fix it by hand, and one row at the bottom fixes it all with your AI assistant.
