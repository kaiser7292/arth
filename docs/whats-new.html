What's new

Better every week.

Arth updates often. Here's what each update changed, newest first. Updates arrive through Google Play and keep all your data.

Arth 4.41.0
4 Nov 2026
LATEST

Search and sort your holdings

  • Demat accounts: switch between your snapshots and your holdings on any demat account.
  • Holdings: search them, and sort by value, invested amount, profit or loss, today's change or name.
  • Today's change: stocks from Zerodha and Angel One now show how much they moved today.
Arth 4.40.0
4 Nov 2026

See your holdings on your demat account

  • Holdings: a demat account linked to your broker now lists everything you hold, with what you invested and your profit or loss.
  • Transfer templates: teach Arth that a bank message is a transfer between your own accounts, so it stays out of your spending and income.
  • Dates and times: Arth reads more ways of writing them, in both messages and payment pictures.
  • Pictures: fewer misread account numbers and amounts.
  • Opening balance warning: you can now dismiss it for each account.
  • Account ledger: long tags no longer run over the amount.
Arth 4.39.5
4 Nov 2026

Choose where your data starts

  • Start date: if your older data is incomplete, Arth can ignore transactions before a date you pick, in Settings > Transactions. Loans, investments and Hisaab stay as they are, and you can undo it any time.
  • First scan: new users can choose this when they pick how far back Arth reads.
  • Transaction Messages: removed a stray line above the first row in Needs you.
Arth 4.39.4
4 Nov 2026

Spending now means spending, everywhere

  • Reports: money you've marked as a fixed deposit, a SIP or a transfer to your own account no longer counts as spending in Merchants, Budget, Insights, forecasts, comparisons and reports. These screens now agree with My Month.
  • Past totals may drop: months that include this kind of money will show lower spending than before. Your transactions haven't changed.
  • Transactions: these rows now appear under Committed instead of Expenses, so nothing drops out of the list.
Arth 4.39.3
4 Nov 2026

Insights opens again

  • Insights: since 4.39.1 the page always said it couldn't load, and Try again did nothing. Your data was never affected.
Arth 4.39.2
4 Nov 2026

Message templates on iPhone, and a calmer app

  • Message templates on iPhone: teach Arth a new message format, see messages it couldn't read, and check scan history in Transaction Messages.
  • Pluxee meal cards: Arth now reads Pluxee wallet messages by itself.
  • Toolbar on more lists: Hisaab people, Holdings, Vault, Reminders and the review queue now have it, and it stays in place when you scroll. Search understands amounts like >5000.
  • Smoother screens on Android: cards use a thin border instead of a shadow, so dark boxes no longer flash during transitions.
  • Tidier wording and pop-ups: buttons and messages read the same way throughout, with Arth's own pop-ups and loading screens.
  • Messages don't vanish: if Arth reads a message but adds nothing, it says so and shows you why.
Arth 4.39.1
4 Nov 2026

Easier broker setup, and fewer surprises

  • Step-by-step broker setup: Angel One, Zerodha and ZebPay now walk you through creating your app, what to enter in each field and getting your TOTP key, with Copy buttons.
  • Clearer sync failures: when Angel One refuses a sync, Arth shows Angel One's own reason.
  • Merchants: press and hold to select, then filter and select all to set one category.
  • Saved views: they now keep their dates, every filter, sort and grouping.
  • Fixes: today's date is now right between midnight and 5:30am, day and group totals take refunds off, and the "% vs previous period" figure uses the right dates.
  • Failed loads: a screen that can't load now says so, instead of looking empty or showing ₹0.
Arth 4.39.0
4 Nov 2026

Tidier cards, faster merchants

  • Set a category for many merchants: select them in Merchants, set one category, and move their earlier transactions too if you like.
  • Cleaner cards: lines now only sit between rows, with none above the first or below the last, across the app.
  • Audit log dates: opens on the last 7 days, with a calendar icon for 30 days, 90 days or a custom range.
  • Transactions: better spacing around the date at the top.
Arth 4.38.0
3 Nov 2026

One toolbar on every list

  • Search, filter, sort, group by: the same bar in the same place on every long list, with a line saying what's on and an × to undo each thing.
  • More tools on more lists: Together can sort. Account ledgers can filter and sort. Budget, Insights and My Month can search and filter. Merchants, accounts and Smart Rules can sort and group.

Arth 4.37.0
3 Nov 2026

Group your lists any way you like

  • Group by: sort any transaction list under headings with totals: date, month, category, merchant, account, payment mode or person. It's in Transactions, Together, Hisaab, account ledgers, My Month, Budget and Insights.
  • Credit icons fixed: credits now show their type's icon in Transactions instead of Other, and the view screen says Type.
  • Change many at once: select credits on the Credits tab and set their type together.
  • Hisaab settlement: a new type that money paid back gets by itself.
  • Smart Rules and search: a rule can now set a credit's type, and searching a type's name finds its credits. Account ledgers show type icons too.
Arth 4.36.0
3 Nov 2026

Make Arth yours

  • Customise Arth: switch off the features, tabs and cards you don't use and they're gone from every screen and from search. Nothing is deleted. Reorder your Home cards too.
  • Splits: an expense now shows who paid, each person's percentage and amount, and who owes whom. One sheet edits it, already filled in.
  • Credits have a type: type replaces category, with an icon for each, plus Dividend and Rent received. Filter and group by type in Transactions.
  • Income month: choose the month an income counts for while adding or editing it.
  • Household sharing: set each category to Always share or Share only when split. Shared spends show the icon of whoever added them.

A more reliable investment sync

  • Fixed: with two or more brokers connected, a sync could fail with an error instead of saving.
  • Accounts you track by hand: enter holdings and idle funds separately, filled in from last time. Change them or carry on.

Insights made simple, and see how your investments moved

Arth 4.28.2
29 Oct 2026

One split, and whoever paid

  • One split, in one place: split a bill with your household from the transaction's own Split, and remove it there; it stays removed. Arth no longer splits anything by itself. Shared with Home is now one switch: whether the household sees a transaction.
  • Paid with someone else's card: record that your partner paid, and their phone finds the matching transaction on their card (or asks which account) and splits it, so their card, budget and Hisaab all agree.
  • Details in Hisaab: entries from your household show the merchant and category, not just an amount.
  • Together, tidier: one Filter for who paid, who owes whom and category; long pickers like category mapping scroll and have search. Monthly totals of personal spending are gone.
  • New versions (Android): Arth tells you when an update is out, from the Play Store or GitHub, wherever you installed it from. Turn it off in Settings.
  • Back on every screen: the Hisaab ledger keeps its back and Home buttons on iPhone.

Arth 4.28
29 Oct 2026

Money, together

  • Households: share spending with your partner or family, up to 5 people. Invite with a QR code or a link; each of you keeps your own Arth and chooses what to share.
  • Bills split themselves: rent, groceries and other household spends are shared and split as you approve them. Mark any transaction shared or personal, split equally, pay it all, or set your own share.
  • Together: what you spent together this month, by person and by category, right from Home. Tap any total for its transactions, and filter by who, what or who owes whom.
  • Their categories, your way: when someone files a bill under a category you don't have, map it to one of yours or create it.
  • Hisaab on both phones: your share of their bills lands in your Hisaab and your budget by itself, settle-ups show on both sides, and Arth tells you when both phones agree.
  • Plan together: agree a household budget, and save towards shared goals from your own investment buckets.
  • Private by design: what you share is locked on your phone before it leaves; Arth's relay can't read it and deletes it once everyone has it.
