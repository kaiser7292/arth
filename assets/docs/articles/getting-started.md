Arth is a private finance tracker that runs entirely on your phone. No account, no cloud, no sign-up. You can use it offline forever.

## The three things to know

1. **Expenses arrive two ways.** Arth reads your bank SMS automatically, or you tap the **+** button and log one yourself.
2. **You review before anything counts.** Auto-detected transactions land in the **Review Queue** on the Home tab. Nothing enters your budget or account balance until you approve it.
3. **Your data is yours.** Any time in **Settings tab → Backup & Storage → Backup & Restore**, you can export an encrypted backup file. Restore it on any device.

## The five tabs

Arth has **five bottom tabs**, in this order from left to right:

- **Home** - today's picture. Month-to-date spend ring, Review Queue, Upcoming Dues, Reminders, recent expenses, and the **Your Accounts** section (Credit Cards, Bank Accounts, Wallets, Demat, Loans - each with live balances and tap-to-drill). Also the entry point to the **Insights screen** (tap the Insights card on Home).
- **Transactions** - the full ledger. Both expenses and credits. Search, filter by date / category / account / tag, toggle between Expenses and Credits. Save your favourite filter combinations as named views (tap "Save view" in the filter panel) and set one as default. Active filter pills show at a glance what's applied.
- **Budget** - monthly caps per category with progress bars. Right-spend (unavoidable) vs discretionary split. Month-End Projection widget.
- **Goals** - savings goals, milestones, investment buckets, yearly plan, year-over-year comparison, salary calculator, balance sheet.
- **Settings** - accounts, categories, payment modes, merchant aliases, smart rules, reminders, SMS settings, backup/restore, fiscal year, security (biometric lock), audit log.

> Insights is not a bottom tab - it opens from the Insights card on Home.

## Your first week (in order)

1. **Onboarding** - pick a theme, set your fiscal year start (default: April for India), grant SMS permission if you want auto-detection.
2. **Add your accounts.** Settings tab → Master Data → Accounts → **+**. Add savings, credit cards, wallets, loans, demat - everything your money moves through.
3. **Turn on SMS detection** (if you skipped it). Settings tab → SMS Detection → **Enable**.
4. **Open the Review Queue** after a day - approve what Arth auto-captured. This is the main loop of Arth.
5. **Set one budget.** Budget tab → tap a category → set a monthly cap. Watch it fill up over the month.
6. **Create your first backup.** Settings tab → Backup & Storage → Backup & Restore → **Create Backup**. Save the `.artha` file somewhere safe (Drive, email to yourself, etc.).

## Common situations

**Review Queue shows 20+ items from old SMS.** Arth scans the last 180 days by default. Go to Review Queue → filter to **Auto-Detected** → tap **Reject All** for anything you don't want, or approve in bulk.

**I don't want SMS detection.** Skip it - SMS is off by default until you enable it. Everything works manually. Tap **+** on Home or Transactions to log.

**iPhone / iPad?** Auto-SMS on iOS is restricted by the OS, so you'd log manually. The rest of Arth works the same.
