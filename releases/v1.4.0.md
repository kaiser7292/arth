## What's new in v1.4.0

### Fixed
- **Milestones**: Investment bucket contributions now correctly appear in the FY breakdown, monthly history, and projection on the milestone detail screen. Previously only direct contributions were counted; linked bucket contributions (manual entries and expense links) were missing from these views, showing ₹0 even when real contributions existed.
- **YoY comparison**: Milestone actuals now include linked bucket contributions, giving accurate year-over-year numbers.
- **Savings tracker & financial cockpit**: Milestone contribution totals now reflect all sources including linked buckets.

### Improved
- Replaced em dashes (—) with hyphens (-) across all screens, help articles, alerts, and labels. Affects ~98 files including onboarding, settings, loans, goals, reconciliation, insights, simulator, hisaab, and all 30 help center articles.
